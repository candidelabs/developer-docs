# Create a Gas Policy

A gas policy is a set of rules that Candide's paymaster checks against each UserOperation. When an operation matches, the paymaster sponsors it, and the gas is paid from the funds held by the policy. Policies are created in the [Dashboard](https://dashboard.candide.dev) or with the [Platform API](/platform/overview).

## Who is This For?

Wallets, stablecoin apps and payment apps use a private policy to pay for their own users' transactions. Most policies are private. The wallet's backend sends the policy ID with each sponsorship request, and the paymaster checks only that policy. [Sponsor Your Users' Gas](/wallet/guides/send-gasless-tx/) has the code.

dApps and protocols use a public policy to pay for actions on their own contracts. A public policy has no ID to pass: the paymaster checks it for requests from any wallet that uses Candide's paymaster. See [Public Gas Policies](/instagas/usecases/).

## Create a Policy

1. Create a policy in the [Dashboard](https://dashboard.candide.dev) or with the [Gas Policy API](/platform/gas-policy-api).
2. Set it to private or public in the General Rules.
3. Add rules for what you want to sponsor. A transaction rule can limit sponsorship to calls on your contracts, and an account rule can cap how much each account uses.
4. Top up your team balance and move funds to the policy.

The policy sponsors operations while it is enabled, funded, and between its start and end dates. The Analytics and UserOperation tabs in the Dashboard show what it has sponsored.

Keep a private policy's ID on your backend. Anyone who has the ID can get operations sponsored from the policy's funds, up to the limits its rules set.

## Rules

Rules fall into four groups: general, account, access and transaction. [Policy Rules](/instagas/gas-policies) describes each rule. To manage policies from code, start with the [Platform API quickstart](/platform/overview).
